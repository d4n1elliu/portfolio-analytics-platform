# CSL: plasma collections recover

*Fictional research note written for this demo. It is not real analysis and not investment advice.*

**Ticker:** CSL
**Date:** 2026-08-20

## Summary

CSL's plasma business is recovering faster than we expected. The cost of collecting each litre of plasma has fallen for three halves in a row, and margins in the main division are close to their level before the pandemic. The vaccines division remains weak.

## Plasma collection

Collection volumes rose 9% on the prior year. The new donation machines, now installed in most centres, shorten each donation and let a centre take more donors a day. Management expects the rollout to finish by the middle of 2027.

Lower cost per litre is the most important number in this result. It feeds through to margins about nine months later, because of the time it takes to turn plasma into finished product.

## Immunoglobulins

Demand for immunoglobulin products stayed strong and the company gained share in the United States. Supply is no longer the constraint it was two years ago.

## Vaccines

Flu vaccine sales fell again as vaccination rates in the United States declined. We have lowered our forecast for this division and no longer assume it returns to growth before 2028.

## Risks

- A competitor's new treatment could reduce demand for immunoglobulins in one of the main conditions they treat.
- Vaccination rates could keep falling.
- Most earnings are in US dollars, so the currency matters.

## Our view

We rate CSL as a buy. The recovery in plasma margins is the main driver, and it has further to run.
