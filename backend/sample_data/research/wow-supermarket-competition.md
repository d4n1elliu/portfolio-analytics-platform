# WOW: supermarket competition and cost of living

*Fictional research note written for this demo. It is not real analysis and not investment advice.*

**Ticker:** WOW
**Date:** 2026-07-15

## Summary

Woolworths is growing sales slowly and giving up some margin to keep prices low. Shoppers remain careful with money, and the company faces close attention from regulators on supermarket pricing.

## Sales

Food sales in Australia rose 2.5% on the prior year, slightly below the rate of food price inflation, which means volumes fell a little. More customers are choosing own-brand products, which sell for less but earn a similar margin.

Online sales grew much faster than store sales and now make up a meaningful share of the total. Delivery remains less profitable than store sales.

## Margins

The gross margin fell as the company lowered prices on several hundred everyday items. Management described this as an investment in customer trust. We expect margins to stay under pressure for at least another year.

## Regulation

The competition regulator's review of supermarket pricing is continuing. New rules on how discounts are displayed are likely. The direct cost should be small, but the review limits the company's ability to raise prices.

## New Zealand and Big W

The New Zealand business improved from a low base. Big W made a small loss, and management is reviewing its future again.

## Our view

We rate WOW as a hold. It is a defensive business in a hard period, and we see no reason to add until margins stop falling.
